Choosing equipment

POS system or payment terminal?

The right choice starts with the job you need done at checkout.

Merchant Pro resource · 3 minute read

A payment terminal focuses on payment.

A terminal is designed to accept payments. Depending on the device and configuration, it may support chip cards, contactless payments, receipts, tips, and other checkout functions.

A POS system connects more of the sale.

A point-of-sale system may combine payment acceptance with functions such as product catalogs, inventory, staff permissions, reporting, or restaurant workflows. Features and subscription costs depend on the product and plan.

What does your business need?

ConsiderQuestions to ask
CheckoutDo you need a simple amount entry, a product catalog, or table and order management?
LocationWill payments happen at a counter, around a room, or away from the business?
ConnectionsWhich software, printers, and reporting tools need to work together?
Ongoing costWhat are the equipment, software, processing, and support charges?

Already have equipment?

Start with the model, software version, and any integrations you rely on. Confirm compatibility, ownership or lease terms, and migration requirements before replacing anything.

See your workflow demonstrated.

Ask to walk through a realistic sale, refund, receipt, and end-of-day report. Include the staff who will use the equipment so you can spot practical questions early.

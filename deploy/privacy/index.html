Privacy notice

This notice describes how to contact Merchant Pro through this website.

Information you provide

Statement-review links let you call Merchant Pro or open your email application. You may choose to provide your name, business name, contact details, and a brief message in an initial email. Do not include card numbers, bank details, passwords, or account identifiers.

How an inquiry is used

Information you send to Merchant Pro is used to respond to your request and discuss Merchant Pro services. Phone and email services process information needed to deliver those communications. This website does not submit or store a statement-review form.

Technical information

The hosting service may process connection information, such as an IP address and browser request details, to serve the website and protect it. This website does not add advertising trackers or marketing analytics.

Contact and requests

For questions about information you have provided, or to request a correction or deletion, contact corpaccounts@merchantproinc.com or call 888-573-1133. Do not send sensitive financial information with your request.
